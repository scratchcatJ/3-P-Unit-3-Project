Smoothie Recipe!:

  • Time: 4 mins
  • Serves: 2 people
Ingredients Quantity
frozen Bluberries 1 1/2 cup
Bannana Single (medium)
Peanut butter #1 1/3 cup
Sheep Yogurt 1/2 cup
Allergen substitutes: Almond butter, Oat milk

Step 1: Place 3/4 cup of frozen bluberries in blender, along with entirety of sheep yogurt (blend until smooth consistency)
Step 2: Add rest of ingredients all together


notice: in the case of jamming, either smash into blender with metal spoon, or add up to 4 tablespoons of water to loosen clumps

Borscht Recipe!:

  • Time: 45 mins
  • Serves: 9 serving
    • Ingredients Quantity
    yellow onion 1
    kielbasa 14 oz
    pickle juice 2 cup
    mini sweet gold potato 7
    cabbage 4 outer leaves
    carrot 1 (large)
    butter 1/4 stick
    beats 1 can

    Step 1: slice onion thinly and fry till sweet and brown with 1/4 stick butter in large pot
    Step 2: Add 1/2 inch slices of kielbasa to pot and bring to fry, wait till kielbasa oil sweats to add 3 cups water

    Step 3: add in diced or thinly sliced carrot once water comes to boil, along with half cut potatoes ~
    Step 4: add in thin sliced cabbage once all ingedients have softened, along with beets and pickle juice
